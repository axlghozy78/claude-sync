# Product copy

Use the user's language and the product's established vocabulary. For Indonesian audiences, write natural Indonesian; keep familiar product terms only when they improve understanding. Do not turn a simple wording edit into a marketing strategy exercise.

Gather the audience, offer, primary action and available proof from the brief or existing materials. Ask only about gaps that change the meaning of the copy. Separate facts from proposals. Never invent customers, testimonials, awards, discounts, scarcity, performance numbers or guarantees.

For a page, establish the message hierarchy before styling: what this is, who it helps, the concrete outcome, why to trust it, and what to do next. Choose the sequence for this product instead of filling a standard SaaS section template. Use the actual product and user's task as the subject of sentences.

Write specific verbs and nouns. Replace unearned superlatives and generic promises such as revolutionary, seamless, next-level, solusi terbaik, and tingkatkan produktivitas with the supported behavior or result. These are editorial cues, not a ban when the user's deliberate brand language calls for them. Avoid vague repeated headlines, decorative English and keyword stuffing.

An action label describes what happens: Simpan perubahan, Lihat jadwal, or Kirim permintaan booking. Preserve the same name through the flow. A form explains needed information at the point of use. An error names the problem and an available recovery action without exposing internals. Empty states help someone take a useful first step.

Review the copy in its actual layout when available: mobile wrapping, button length, scan order, contrast between primary and secondary actions, and whether surrounding content answers the visitor's next question. Cut repetition and unsupported claims in one focused editing pass, then fix any observed layout issues. Do not prescribe endless scoring or stylistic rewrites.

Return publishable copy in context. Flag missing facts outside the proposed copy; use clearly labeled placeholders only if they are unavoidable. Do not publish or send it externally unless that action is authorized.
